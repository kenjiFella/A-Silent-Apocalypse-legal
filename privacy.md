2. Privacy Policy
Last Updated: 5Th of August 2026.

Kenji&Dalmatinski Games built A Silent Apocalypse As a completely offline game. This Privacy Policy outlines our data collection practices.

1. Zero Data Collection
A Silent Apocalypse is a fully offline, singleplayer game. We do not collect, store, track, transmit, or share any personal information, usage metrics, device information, or gameplay telemetry.

No Accounts: You are not required to register, sign in, or provide an email address.

No Analytics: The Game does not use third-party analytics trackers, crash reporters, or background network calls.

No Cookies / Online Trackers: The Game does not connect to the internet during execution.

2. Save Data & Local Files
All game progression, settings, and save files are stored locally on your device by GameMaker. We do not have access to your local save files or your device's filesystem.

3. Distribution Platforms
If you download the Game through a third-party platform (such as Steam, itch.io, or the Epic Games Store), that platform may collect account, download, or installer data according to its own privacy policy. We do not control or have access to personal data gathered by distribution platforms.
